Payment Plans
How payment plans work
Break your balance into smaller monthly payments that fit your budget. Always 0% interest, never a fee.
What you get
  • Split your balance into 3, 6, or 12 monthly payments, or choose a custom monthly amount.
  • Always 0% interest with no setup or hidden fees.
  • The first installment is charged when you start the plan; the rest are charged on the 1st of each month.
  • A reminder email arrives 7 days before every installment.
Automatic payments
Installments are charged automatically to your saved payment method. If a charge doesn't go through, we'll email you and retry over the next two days — and you can update your payment method anytime from My Payment Plans.
Pay off early, anytime
You can pay your remaining plan balance at any time with no penalty. The plan simply closes once the balance reaches zero.
